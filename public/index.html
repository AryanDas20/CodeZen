Features

Four parts that work together

Each part has its own page in the workspace, with a plain-language explanation beside every function.

Brand memory

Store guidelines, approved facts, terminology rules and policies. Upload PDF or Word files, or paste text. Reviews only rely on what you add.

Content review

Paste a draft and pick its channel. The checker finds absolute promises, superlatives, numbers and banned words, and shows exactly where.

Explainable findings

Each finding has the excerpt, a severity, the reason it matters, a possible matching source and a suggested fix.

Human approval

A reviewer approves, requests changes or rejects, with a reason. Every decision lands in a dated audit trail. Nothing is published automatically.

How it works

From draft to decision in three steps

Start with a guest session to see every screen, then create an account to keep your work.

1

Add approved sources

Put your real guidelines and facts in Brand memory so checks have something trustworthy to compare against.

2

Review a real draft

Paste the text, read each finding and its evidence, and edit the draft. Each edit is saved as a new version.

3

Record the decision

Approve after verifying the evidence, or send it back with a reason. The audit trail keeps the record.
